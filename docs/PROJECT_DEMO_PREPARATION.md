# Project demo: questions to practise

These are likely questions for the editable Merit Lens sample, not a fixed script. The live interview chooses questions from your uploaded description and follows your answers. Standard oral defence normally takes 3-6 questions in total.

1. What problem does Merit Lens solve?
2. Why ask students about their work instead of just reading it?
3. Why change one small detail after asking them to explain a choice?
4. Why use a talking interviewer? What happens if the voice or camera fails?
5. Who decides the final grade, and what does the AI help with?
6. What can go wrong in this prototype, and how would you improve it?

Possible follow-ups: Can you give an example? What would you change? How would you check that? A different answer can lead to a different follow-up; these are not extra guaranteed questions.

To test the report, try one deliberately wrong claim, such as: "A camera warning proves cheating" or "The AI makes the final grading decision without the teacher." The report should record the claim and explain the problem using the submitted description. It should also record any later correction.

No final grade is configured for the project sample. You can test the evidence report directly; upload grading criteria if you also want a suggested numerical grade.
