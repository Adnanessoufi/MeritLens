Reading-list application

Build a Python reading-list application that stores books by ISBN, supports adding and finding books, and handles duplicate ISBNs and missing books safely.
Explain the data structure choice, average and worst-case lookup complexity, and test cases for duplicates, missing books and empty input.
Grading criteria: correctness 50 points; explanation of design and trade-offs 30 points; requirements and tests 20 points.
Important concepts: dictionaries, search complexity, testing.
